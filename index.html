The dates we keep for you.

Most penalties come from a missed date, not a wrong number. On a monthly package we track every one of these and file before it is due.

7thevery month
PAYE, SDL and withholding taxReturns and payment for the previous month's payroll and payments.
20thevery month
VAT return and paymentDue for the previous month, even when no VAT is payable.
3 monthsinto the year
Provisional tax returnYour estimated tax for the year, then paid in four quarterly instalments.
6 monthsafter year-end
Final income tax returnFor a December year-end, that means 30 June, with any balance of tax paid.

Dates follow the Tanzania Revenue Authority calendar. When a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, check TRA's published calendar for the exact day.

Questions we hear often.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp or book a consultation.

What does LinkEdge Advisory do?

We are a Dar es Salaam firm that brings tax, accounting and legal advice together. A tax expert, a CPA accountant and an advocate work on the same file, so your filings, books and contracts all agree with each other.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the size of your business and the work involved. We offer monthly packages and fixed fees for single jobs. After a short review of your needs we send a written quote, and no work begins until you have agreed to it.

What should I bring to our first meeting?

Whatever you have of the following: your BRELA registration certificate, TIN certificate, recent financial statements or bank statements, any letters or assessments from TRA, and the contracts you want us to look at. If you are just starting a business, bring your idea and the names of the owners.

I have received a TRA assessment or audit notice. Can you help?

Yes. We review the assessment, prepare objections, respond to audit findings and handle correspondence with TRA for you. Objections have strict time limits, so contact us as soon as you receive the notice.

When are the monthly tax deadlines?

PAYE, SDL and withholding tax returns are due by the 7th of the following month. VAT returns and payment are due by the 20th of the following month. The calendar at the top of this page shows the next dates.

Do I need to file a VAT return if I had no sales?

Yes. A VAT-registered business must submit its monthly return even when no VAT is payable for that month. Missing it can still lead to penalties.

Can you register my company and get my TIN?

Yes. We handle company incorporation and business name registration with BRELA, TIN and VAT registration, and business licences. How long it takes depends on BRELA and TRA processing; we tell you what to expect before we start.

Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. Your records are handled only by the team working on your file and are never shared without your permission, except where the law requires it.
