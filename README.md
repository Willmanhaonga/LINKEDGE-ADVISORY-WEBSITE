# LinkEdge Advisory Limited — website

One-page website for LinkEdge Advisory Limited, a tax, accounting and legal advisory firm in Dar es Salaam.

- `index.html`: the page
- `assets/site.css`: styles
- `assets/site.js`: menu, TRA deadline calendar, consultation form
- `assets/fonts`, logos and icons
- `DESIGN.md`: colours, fonts and motion rules

## Publish free with GitHub Pages
Settings → Pages → Source: "Deploy from a branch" → Branch: `main` / root → Save.
The site will appear at `https://willmanhaonga.github.io/LINKEDGE-ADVISORY-WEBSITE/`.

## Connect the consultation form to email
Open `assets/site.js` and put your form service address (for example a Formspree endpoint) in `FORM_ENDPOINT`. Until then, the form sends the visitor's details to WhatsApp (+255 719 856 488).
